# About

## What Noctaxis is for

Noctaxis is a desktop planning application for photography and astrophotography, designed to help you make informed decisions before heading out with your equipment.

It's the first step in planning a shoot: deciding where to go, when to go, and what you can realistically expect from the conditions.

Explore potential locations, move through dates and times, preview compositions using your own camera equipment, and examine how terrain, weather, moonlight and light pollution might affect your plans.

More importantly, Noctaxis helps you understand the compromises involved. Would waiting another hour improve your target's visibility? Is travelling to a darker location worthwhile? Would another night offer better conditions, and what might you sacrifice by going sooner?

It's not intended to replace capture software, telescope control, tracking or alignment tools. Noctaxis focuses on the planning that comes before those things, rather than attempting to manage the entire photography process.

Designed primarily for use at home, Noctaxis also supports touchscreens, allowing you to revisit and adjust your plans on a rugged tablet or other portable Windows, Linux or macOS device when you're away from your desk.

The goal is simple: **help you decide what, where and when to photograph, before committing to the journey.**

## Why I built Noctaxis

I've always enjoyed astrophotography, but I approach it primarily with a camera rather than a telescope.

When looking for planning software, I found that many applications were designed around telescope-based workflows. Some are flexible enough to accommodate both, but that often means navigating a complicated application just to use a small fraction of its features.

I wanted something simpler, without sacrificing the tools that actually matter.

I also wanted an application that felt modern and worked comfortably on a touchscreen. My ideal workflow is to plan a shot on my PC or Toughbook, then export it as an image to keep on my phone. Before I even leave, I can check the evening's weather, see where my target should be when I arrive, and work out the composition using my own camera and lenses.

Once I'm on location, Noctaxis has already done its job. I can refer to my plan, use Stellarium on my phone to navigate the sky, and set up my tracker and camera manually. I shouldn't need to dig through fifteen submenus just to prepare for a photograph.

Then there's the question of cost. I understand why developers charge for their software, whether through upfront purchases or subscriptions. But I don't believe essential planning features need to be locked behind paywalls. I wanted Noctaxis to remain useful without requiring someone to pay to unlock its core functionality.

Finally, I wanted something that reflected how I actually approach photography.

Not everyone has the luxury of dark skies outside their back door. Even living in a small town, travelling somewhere darker can make a considerable difference. For me, planning isn't just about *when* to photograph something, but *where*, what equipment to bring, and whether the conditions will make the journey worthwhile.

That's what I wanted Noctaxis to do.

**A focused photography planner, without unnecessary complexity, endless menus or paywalls around the tools that matter.**

Noctaxis was my answer.

## Understanding your plan

Noctaxis combines astronomical calculations, terrain information and weather forecasts to help you make informed decisions.

The Planner lets you explore different times, positions and compositions. Shot viability provides an advisory assessment of the focused target, highlighting favourable conditions, potential problems and missing information.

These assessments are guides, not guarantees. Weather forecasts change, terrain models cannot represent every obstruction, and some information may be unavailable. Always consider the actual conditions when planning your shoot.
